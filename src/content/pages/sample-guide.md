## A place to begin :)

This is **sample content**. Imagine a small group gathering around a table to
collect ideas. A notebook and a few questions are enough to get started.

The first conversation can stay _simple_: what would we like to learn, and what
would help us take the next step?

## Bring a few ideas

- A question you would like to explore.
- A short note about something you have noticed.
- One idea to discuss with the group.

### Leave room for a next step

After the conversation, write a short paragraph about what you want to revisit.
There is no fixed schedule or promised outcome in this sample.
